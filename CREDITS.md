# Credits

Omaquarium was written and illustrated by Gavin Nugent, and everything it
installs is covered by the project's MIT [LICENCE](LICENSE).

## What ships in the tank

- **The reef, sand, rock bridge and plants** — modelled and textured for this
  project in Blender, then baked to the meshes and maps the plugin loads.
- **The animals** — clownfish, regal blue tang, lyretail anthias, banggai
  cardinalfish, copperband butterflyfish, royal gramma, purple firefish,
  moorish idol, emperor angelfish, mandarin dragonet, red lionfish, a zebra
  moray, a giant clam and the reef companions — each modelled, rigged and
  animated for this project.
- **The tiki ornament and the aquarium backdrop** — original artwork for this
  project.
- **The Bit mode scene** — original pixel artwork for this project, recoloured
  at runtime from your active Omarchy theme.

Species appearances and material finishes were built from reference material as
colour and surface studies; the shipped meshes and maps are reconstructions
authored here, not captured or purchased assets. No third-party model, texture,
photograph or sound file is redistributed by this plugin, and it bundles no
dependencies beyond the Qt libraries it asks your system for.

The Omarchy wordmark appears engraved on the tiki ornament, as a nod to the
desktop the aquarium was made for. It remains the property of the Omarchy
project and is used here as decoration, not as a claim of endorsement.

## Software it runs on

Omaquarium is a plugin for the Omarchy desktop shell and is built on
[Quickshell](https://quickshell.org) and Qt 6 (Qt Quick 3D), which it requires
at runtime but does not include.
